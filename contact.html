FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our barbershop services.

Do I need an appointment to get a haircut?

No, we welcome walk-ins for all our services. However, during peak hours, wait times may be longer. You can always call ahead to check current availability.

What services do you offer?

We offer a full range of men's grooming services including signature haircuts, beard trims and sculpting, hot towel head shaves, hair coloring, and scalp treatments. Check our Services page for details.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee. Please call us if you need to make changes to your booking.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes! Gift cards are available for purchase in-store or online. They make the perfect gift for any occasion. Contact us for more details.
