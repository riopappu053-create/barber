Insider Tips

Make the Most of Your Walk-In

Pro tips to help you get the best experience when you drop by without an appointment.

Come Early

We open at 9am. The first hour is usually the quietest — get in and out quickly.

Weekdays vs Weekends

Tuesdays & Wednesdays are typically less busy. Saturdays are our busiest day.

Call Ahead

Give us a ring 15–20 minutes before you arrive to check availability and hold your spot.

Group Visits

For groups of 3 or more, we recommend booking in advance to ensure we can seat you together.

Know What You Want

Have a style in mind or bring a photo. This helps our barbers deliver exactly what you're looking for.

Enjoy the Wait

We offer complimentary coffee, water, and Wi-Fi while you wait. Make yourself at home.

FAQ

Walk-In Questions

Everything you need to know about our walk-in policy. If you don't see your question, just ask!

Do I need an appointment to get a haircut?
No, we welcome walk-ins for all our services. However, during peak hours (12–2pm and Saturdays), wait times may be longer. You can always call ahead to check current availability.
What is the average wait time for walk-ins?
Typically 15–30 minutes, but it can vary. We recommend checking our live wait time indicator on this page or calling us before you come.
Can I request a specific barber as a walk-in?
Yes! If your preferred barber is available, we'll seat you with them. If they're busy, you can wait or choose another talented barber from our team.
Are walk-ins more expensive than appointments?
No, our prices are the same whether you walk in or book in advance. We believe in transparent pricing for everyone.
What if I have a large group — can we all walk in?
For groups of 3 or more, we strongly recommend booking ahead to ensure we have enough barbers available to seat you together. Give us a call and we'll sort it out.
Do you take walk-ins for beard trims and shaves?
Absolutely! Beard trims and hot towel shaves are available on a walk-in basis. Wait times may be slightly longer for shaves as they require more time and attention.

Still have questions? Contact us — we're happy to help.
