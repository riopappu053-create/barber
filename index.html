More Than a Cut —
An Experience

Founded in 2012, Blade & Beard was born from a simple belief: every man deserves a place where he can relax, reconnect, and leave looking his absolute best. Our barbers are masters of their craft, blending time-honored techniques with contemporary trends.

From the moment you step through our doors, you're welcomed into a space that feels both timeless and modern — rich wood, leather chairs, and the unmistakable hum of clippers. We use only premium products and take the time to understand your personal style.
